Cat

Fossil records show cats were domesticated over 9,000 years ago in the Near East. Cats and other felines, along with lions and tigers, are regarded as having a single evolutionary lineage and at one point in the past shared a common ancestor.

      Scientific Name: Felis catus
      Lifespan: 12-16 years

Cats live in homes and in the wild and you must be careful because some of the cats can scratch you.
